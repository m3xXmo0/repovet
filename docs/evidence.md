# Evidence

What repovet detects, what it misses and how often it raises false alarms. All sets
below are small gap lists that show where repovet is blind; they are not detection-rate
estimates. The reconstructions of documented attacks and their variants are built
alongside the rules, so they show what the rules are built for, not how well they
generalize; the sealed blind list and the partly independent holdout therefore come
first. In every report, read "Not checked" first.

## Sealed blind list

**Sealed blind list** (30 attack variants and 30 harmless cases, sealed before the first evaluation; measured with 0.5.0): 13 rated as expected, 2 detected lower or in another area, 15 of 30 missed; 25 of 30 harmless cases stay below medium.

A fresh blind gap list was sealed before its first evaluation, with criteria fixed
in advance: no new medium-or-higher false alarms, newly missed attacks or rating
regressions against the frozen baseline. On 30 attack variants, 0.5.0 rates 13 as
expected, detects 2 lower or in another area, and misses 15. Of 30 harmless cases,
25 stay below medium and 5 trigger findings. The regression gate passes without
new regressions. This is a small gap list, not a general detection-rate estimate.

## Older holdout

**Older holdout** (42 bypass variants and 24 harmless look-alikes written by a third party; partly independent since 0.4.1; measured with 0.5.0): 13 rated as expected, 13 detected lower or in another area, 16 missed; 23 of 24 look-alikes stay below medium.

A separate, unpublished set of 42 bypass variants and 24 harmless look-alikes was
written by a third party before 0.4.1. The recorded result for 0.4.0 is 11 variants
rated at the expected severity, 13 detected at medium or above but rated lower or in
another area, and 18 missed; 22 of the 24 look-alikes stay below medium. On the same
set, 0.4.1 rates 13 at the expected severity, detects 13 lower or in another area, and
misses 16; 23 of the 24 look-alikes stay below medium. Workflow cases remain weak (0 of
6 rated). The earlier results informed the technique classes targeted by 0.4.1, so this
is now a regression check and only partly independent. Version 0.5.0 has the same
result.

**False positives: the 50 most-starred GitHub repositories** (repovet 0.5.0, 2026-11-02)

38 scanned offline with all rules, 12 skipped (too large to clone or failed; listed in the report). 3 of 38 had at least one high or critical finding. Findings: 0 critical, 5 high, 62 medium, 58 low, 448 info. These projects are presumed benign, so every finding is a false positive or something the project does on purpose.

Rules that fire most often (repositories with a finding): RC-PROV-010 (info, 22 of 38), RC-AGENT-001 (info, 13 of 38), RC-BIN-006 (low, 6 of 38), RC-AUTO-002 (low, 5 of 38), RC-DLX-005 (medium, 3 of 38).

Per rule and per repository: [docs/fp-benchmark.md](https://github.com/m3xXmo0/repovet/blob/main/docs/fp-benchmark.md).

**No findings does not mean safe.** repovet reports only what its rules detect in the checked scope; the known gaps above are examples of what it misses.
